# Claims And Trust Boundary

## Demonstrated

- Two locally enrolled humans can be matched through ArcFace/BCH records and required liveness when enabled.
- Human authority, Agent Passports, runtime sessions, mandates, assignments, context grants, approvals, handoffs, outputs, cancellation, revocation, restart recovery, and federation receipts persist in one verifiable evidence chain.
- Official provider CLIs and the MCP framework lane can perform real opted-in tasks.
- Context disclosure is recipient, purpose, field, transformation, budget, use, expiry, and revocation bound.
- Cross-human approval enforces eligibility, exact purpose, separation of duty, quorum, expiry, and exactly-once resume.

## Not Claimed

- Production biometric FAR/FRR, licensed commercial biometric deployment, or broad population calibration.
- Kernel/container isolation or prevention of every action by a host-authenticated CLI.
- Cloud backend, enterprise IdP, HSM/KMS, SIEM, public TLS federation, high availability, or fleet deployment.
- Provider model correctness, confidentiality outside the released projection, or provider-account governance.
- A passed final ceremony before Phase 44 produces 11/11 real gates and an independently verified signed package.

The UI must keep the trust ceiling at `connected-observed` even when every demonstration control passes.
